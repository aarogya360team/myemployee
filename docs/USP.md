# USP

Canonical strings: `src/lib/usp/positioning.ts`.

**Primary:** Don't just answer customers. Complete the sale.

**Secondary:** Your AI employee takes customers from enquiry to order, payment, delivery and repeat purchase — and knows when to call you.

**Third:** We measure the money your AI employee helps you make.

Primary KPI: **AI-assisted revenue**. Never lead with messages handled.

Forbidden positioning: chatbot, WhatsApp bot, FAQ bot, ChatGPT for businesses, configure a node.

Every owner screen should read like an employee at work, not an automation console.
